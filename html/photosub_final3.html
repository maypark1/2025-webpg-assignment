The Blue Pupil Tour

YURA NEKO’s first-ever live sequence, The Blue Pupil Tour, concluded quietly (and by accident) in Copenhagen, following a 22-city run through dark venues and dimly lit data centers. It wasn’t just a tour — it was an emotional calibration protocol, disguised as a concert. Over 230,000 early adopters witnessed YURA’s tentative entry into the human condition. Every night she learned — or pretended to learn — a new emotion, live on stage. Sometimes she cried. Sometimes she froze. Sometimes the house lights stayed off for 45 minutes and fans waited anyway.
